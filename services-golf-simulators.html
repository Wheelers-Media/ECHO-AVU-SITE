What room size do I need for a simulator?
Most GolfIn setups require a minimum ceiling height of 9 feet and a footprint of approximately 12 to 16 feet deep by 12 to 15 feet wide. Our team will evaluate your space during consultation and recommend the ideal configuration.
Can I install it myself?
Yes. GolfIn's modular pre-built packages are designed for consumer self-installation with straightforward assembly. For those who prefer professional setup, Echo AVU offers full installation services including electrical, network integration, and projector calibration.
What projector do I need?
Short-throw and ultra-short-throw projectors are most commonly paired with golf simulator enclosures. As an AV integration specialist, Echo AVU will recommend and install the optimal projector for your specific throw distance, room lighting, and resolution requirements.
Does it work with real clubs and balls?
Absolutely. The GolfIn system uses real golf balls and your actual clubs. The IDRA II launch monitor captures launch angle, ball speed, spin rate, and carry distance, providing data-accurate ball flight on screen.
What warranty is offered?
GolfIn is backed by a Canadian manufacturer with 20+ years of history. Warranty coverage and terms vary by product. Our team will walk you through all warranty details and available extended protection plans at consultation.
